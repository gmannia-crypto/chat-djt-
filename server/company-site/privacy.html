2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate the Service and improve your experience.

2.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Username: When you participate in leaderboards, daily challenges, or the Political Arena, you may provide a username (e.g., "@MAGAMike"). This is stored locally and on our servers.
  • User Content: When you use features like "Roast Me," "Therapy," "Fortune Parlor," or "Suggest an Idea," your inputs (questions, answers, suggestions) are stored to personalize your experience and improve our AI.
  • Location (Optional): If you choose to join the Political Arena conversation, you may provide your city, state, and country. This is used only to personalize welcome messages and is not shared.
  • Email (Optional): If you contact us for support or sign up for notifications, we may collect your email address.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: We may collect your device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address for analytics and to improve performance.
  • Usage Data: We track which features you use (e.g., therapy sessions, sports picks, property searches) to understand what users enjoy and to award badges and leaderboard points.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use browser local storage to remember your preferences (e.g., sound settings, streak count, badge progress, token balance).

2.3 Third-Party Services

Our Service integrates with the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Affiliate Links: When you click on affiliate links to sportsbooks (e.g., BetMGM, DraftKings), lenders (e.g., Quicken Loans, Rocket Mortgage), or retailers (e.g., Barstool Sports, Fanatics), those third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.
  • Google Maps: The Real Estate section uses Google Maps. Google's privacy policy applies to any location data you enter.
  • Analytics: We may use simple analytics (e.g., console logs) to track feature usage. We do not currently use Google Analytics or similar third-party trackers, but may add them in the future with notice.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide the Service: Operate leaderboards, badges, daily challenges, mystery box, therapy memory, and other interactive features.
  • To Personalize Your Experience: Remember your username, streak, badges, and preferences across sessions.
  • To Improve the Service: Analyze usage patterns to add new features, fix bugs, and optimize performance.

Retention: We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us (see Section 10).
