Make Every Interaction Shareable

The Opportunity: The funniest debates, roasts, and fortunes are currently trapped in the app.

The Action: For every major interaction (debate result, fortune, roast), add a prominent "Share This" button that generates a unique, branded image or video clip with the user's result and a call-to-action to download the app. This is your single most powerful free marketing tool.

Guide New Users Immediately

The Opportunity: A first-time user might feel overwhelmed by 20+ personas and a dozen features.

The Action: Add a brief, optional tutorial or a "Quick Start" guide when a user first launches the app. Highlight the "Try It Now" demo on your landing page to let users sample the core debate before installing.
